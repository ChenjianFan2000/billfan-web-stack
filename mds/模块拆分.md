```
[M1 db]
   ↓
[M2 auth] ──────────────────────────┐
   ↓                                 │
[M3 categories] [M4 papers]          │
        ↑             ↑              │
     [M5 upload] ──→ [M6 pipeline]   │
        ↓                            │
     [M7 logger]  ←──────────────────┘
        ↑

        ↓

```
